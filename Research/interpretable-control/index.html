Interpretable Learning-Based Control

Contents
  1. Overview

  3. Publications

Model predictive control laws learned as oblique decision trees, over 1,000× faster than online MPC.

Overview

Model predictive control (MPC) performs well but must solve an optimization problem online at every step. The control law of linear MPC is piecewise affine, so it can be represented exactly by an oblique decision tree with linear predictions at the leaf nodes. Building on this, I learn MPC laws as interpretable decision trees offline and deploy the tree online as the controller.

Results

The resulting controllers perform competitively with MPC at the speed of a low-level PID controller: over 1,000× faster than MPC and 10× faster than neural network equivalents. I have extended this framework to quadcopter control and to interpretable reinforcement learning for multi-loop adaptive PID control.
