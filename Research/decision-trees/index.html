Training decision trees close to optimality on up to ten million samples.

Overview

I work on algorithms that train decision trees close to optimality: a scalable deterministic global optimization algorithm for optimal decision trees, GPU-accelerated moving-horizon methods that train deep classification trees on up to ten million samples, and a differentiable “ReLU+Argmin” reformulation of decision trees for gradient-based training.

From the Papers

Code

The moving-horizon method is available as the open-source Julia package MHDETrees.jl (MIT License).
