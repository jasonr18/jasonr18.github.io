I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Chemical and Biological Engineering at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, working with Prof. Styliani Avraamidou and Prof. Victor M. Zavala. I received my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from the University of British Columbia in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Yankai Cao, and my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees from the College of Control Science and Engineering at Zhejiang University in 2018 and 2021 under the supervision of Prof. Dong Ni.

My research develops optimization algorithms and interpretable machine learning for decision-making in process, energy, and manufacturing systems. My current work focuses on decision-focused and surrogate-based optimization: designing surrogates specifically for optimization problems such as single-level, bilevel, multi-level, two-stage stochastic, and multi-stage programs. During my Ph.D., I built global optimization algorithms that train interpretable models (clustering and optimal decision trees) on up to one billion samples, and used oblique decision trees to learn model predictive control laws that run over 1,000× faster than online MPC.

Research Interests

  • Decision-focused and surrogate-based optimization.
  • Global optimization algorithms for large-scale interpretable machine learning.
  • Learning-based control with interpretable models.
  • Data-driven modeling, monitoring, and control of manufacturing, energy, and process systems.
